Tracefs verification of SLIDE_TRACEFS_WORKER_CALLER_OFF
=======================================================

Target : SM-S928B, S928BXXU6EZIF
Kernel : 6.1.162-android14-11-34343818-abS928BXXU6EZIF
Method : enable sched:sched_blocked_reason, capture per_cpu/*/trace_pipe_raw for
         three seconds while repeatedly snapshotting /sys/kernel/tracing/trace,
         then decode the raw pages with tools/parse_raw.py.
Access : unprivileged shell user (group readtracefs). No root.

Event ID, read directly from the kernel:

  $ cat /sys/kernel/tracing/events/sched/sched_blocked_reason/id
  106

The text view names the frame but hides the address, which is why a raw capture
is required:

  sched_blocked_reason: pid=28167 iowait=0 caller=worker_thread+0xa0/0x430

It is also the dominant caller on an idle phone: 3011 of 8124 lines in an
earlier four-second capture, and 9930 of 15 repeated snapshots in this one.

Raw decode, 358 events from CPU0 and CPU2:

                caller    image_off  count  worker match
  0xffffffc0081cb39c 0x00001cb39c    147  YES slide=0xf0000
  0xffffffc008258fb8 0x0000258fb8     89  no
  0xffffffc005658c40 0xfffffffffd658c40     70  no
  0xffffffc005d9626c 0xfffffffffdd9626c     25  no
  0xffffffc008258354 0x0000258354      8  no
  0xffffffc0082e4564 0x00002e4564      7  no
  0xffffffc005d11a1c 0xfffffffffdd11a1c      6  no
  0xffffffc005d96e9c 0xfffffffffdd96e9c      5  no
  0x0000000000000000 0x3ff8000000      1  no   (padding, not an event)

One address satisfies the 64 KiB slide grid against the SM-S928N worker offset:

  0x1cb39c - 0xdb39c = 0xf0000        aligned, inside 0..0x1f0000

The check only uses the low 16 bits, which no KASLR slide can change:
0x1cb39c & 0xffff == 0xdb39c & 0xffff == 0xb39c, and no other captured caller
shares that value. 147 of 358 raw events carry it, which is what an idle
kworker blocked in worker_thread should produce.

What this proves: on this kernel the worker_thread blocking site is consistent
with the inherited 0xdb39c offset, so the tracefs slide route is plausible here.

What this does not prove: that 0xdb39c is exactly this Image's instruction
address. A slide makes any offset in the same 64 KiB bucket look identical, and
the text trace cannot be tied to individual raw events because both views
consume or replay the same ring buffer. The firmware extraction disassembles
worker_thread and resolves it directly; this capture is the runtime
cross-check for that result, not a substitute.

Not recorded here on purpose: the boot's slide value, device serial, and
boot_id. The slide is per-boot and the other two identify the handset.
